<div class="container home-page"><h1 class="visually-hidden">GEXIS2D</h1><section class="project-about" aria-label="Software introduction"><p>GEXIS is a finite element method software platform designed for applications in both industry and academia, including geotechnical engineering, off-road mobility, and robotics. One of its key strengths is its comprehensive library of <strong>soil constitutive models</strong>, ranging from classical elastoplastic formulations to advanced models including creep and cyclic loading responses.</p><p>Beyond conventional engineering analysis, GEXIS provides advanced computational capabilities for academic research, including <strong>coupled finite element–discrete element modelling</strong> and the integration of <strong>AI-assisted constitutive models</strong>. With its extensible architecture, GEXIS aims to bridge practical engineering applications and cutting-edge research in computational geomechanics.</p></section><section class="case-browser"
